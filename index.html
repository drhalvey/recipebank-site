What's in the tin

No. 1

Save it from anywhere

A website, a YouTube video, an Instagram, TikTok or Facebook post, a screenshot or a photo of a cookbook page. No ads, no life story, just the recipe.

No. 2

Swap anything

Out of buttermilk? Allergic to nuts? Coriander haters at the table? Tap the ingredient. The method and the nutrition change with it.

No. 3

Make it…

Dairy-free, gluten-free, high protein, keto, vegan, kid-friendly, cheaper or quicker, in one tap. Every change is listed so nothing sneaks past you.

No. 4

Cook hands-free

Big steps, read aloud. Say 'next', 'back' or 'repeat' with flour on your hands. The timers start themselves.

No. 5

Shop for it

A list of what to actually buy, with a rough cost, then your Coles or Woolworths trolley right inside the app.

No. 6

Ideas when you're stuck

Chicken, spinach and feta? Photograph the fridge, or pick an occasion: kids' party, staff morning tea, a quiet night in.

Three dishes, one dinner time.

Pick what's for dinner and when you want to eat. Recipe Bank works backwards and hands you one plan, so nothing's cold and nothing's waiting.
